## <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tarikul-Islam-Anik/Animated-Fluent-Emojis/master/Emojis/People/Technologist.png" alt="Technologist" width="30" /> About Me

Data Science engineering student at **INSEA** (Rabat, Morocco), with a French *classes préparatoires* (MP) background. I work at the intersection of three things:

- **Quantitative finance** — volatility modeling, market risk, time series. I built the first anticipated-risk index for the Moroccan stock market during my internship at EcofinanceAnalytics.
- **Machine learning & AI** — from classical models to NLP and multi-agent systems. Three national hackathon wins in 2026.
- **Data engineering & analytics** — pipelines, cleaning, dashboards. A model nobody can read is a model nobody uses.

What connects them is a habit from prépa: I don't stop at calling a library. I want to be able to rebuild the method and know which assumptions break when the data gets ugly.

> *If something takes your time, it should give you value — or at least curiosity.*
